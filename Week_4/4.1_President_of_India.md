# President of India

1. Articles 52 to 61- President is the highest constitutional authority and the head of the Union Executive.
   a. Article 52 - position of president
   b. Article 53 - executive power of the Union
   c. Articles 54, 55- Election process of the president with representation from parliament and states
   d. Article 56-Term of office
   e. Article 57- eligibility for re-election
   f. Article 59 specifies the conditions under which the President shall serve.
   g. Article 60- President takes an oath to preserve,protect and defend the Constitution and the law.
   h. Article 61-impeachment of the President
2. Role of the President
   a. Head of State, supreme commander of the armed forces, guardian of the constitution.
   b. Law, appointment, and every ordinance flow through their name
   c. Acts on the advice of the council of ministers
3. Executive Powers
   a. The president appoints the prime minister, council of ministers, governors of states, judges of supreme court and high courts, and ambassadors.
4. Legislative Powers
   a. Summons and dissolves Parliament
   b. Address both houses
   c. Give assent to bills
   d. Article 123-President can issue ordinances when Parliament is not in session
5. Financial Powers
   a. Money bill cannot be introduced without the President’s recommendation
   b. The Union Budget is presented in the president’s name, and the president appoints a financial commission every 5 years.
6. Judicial Powers
   a. Power to grant pardons, reprieves, or remissions of punishment
7. Diplomatic & Military powers
   a. President can declare war or peace
8. Emergency Powers
   a. Article 352- National emergency for war or rebellion
   b. Article 365 - President’s rule in states
   c. Article 360 - Financial Emergency
