Constitution of India – NPTEL

Welcome to this repository dedicated to the NPTEL course – Constitution of India.

This repository contains lecture notes, assignment questions and answers, and other study materials to help students understand the Indian Constitution and prepare for course assessments.

📚 Contents

- Notes: Summaries of important topics and constitutional principles.
- Assignments: Weekly assignment questions and answers.
- Study Materials: Key concepts, constitutional provisions, and revision resources.

🎯 Purpose

To create a centralized, accessible resource for students pursuing the NPTEL Constitution of India course, making learning and revision easier.

⚠️ Disclaimer

These materials are intended for educational and reference purposes only. Students are encouraged to verify the answers, refer to official NPTEL materials, and develop their own understanding of the subject.

🤝 Contributions

Suggestions, corrections, and additional learning resources are welcome to improve this repository.
