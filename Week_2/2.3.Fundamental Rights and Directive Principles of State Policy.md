# Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles of State Policy

1. Right to Equality
   - a. Article 14- everyone is equal before the law and gets equal protection of the laws.
   - b. Article 15
      - i. No discrimination in public spaces or institutions
      - ii. Allow scholarships or reservations.
   - c. Article 16
      - i. Equal opportunity in public employment
      - ii. Reservations for backward classes or from underrepresented regions.
   - d. Article 17 - Abolishes untouchability
   - e. Article 18 - Abolishes titles (Shri, Raja, Maharaja,..)
   - f. Article 15(4) and 16(4) - Create special provisions for socially and educationally backward classes
2. Right to Freedom
   - a. Article 19-freedom of speech and expression, assembly, association, movement, residence, and profession.
   - b. Article 20 - Innocent until proven guilty
      - i. No ex post facto laws
      - ii. No double jeopardy
      - iii. No self-incrimination
   - c. Article 21 - Personal liberty is protected under fair, established legal process.
   - d. Article 22- Arrest/Detainment
      - i. When arrested, police should state reason
      - ii. Ability to consult and to be defended by a lawyer of your choice
      - iii. Produced before a magistrate within 24 hours
3. Right Against Exploitation
   - a. Article 23 - Prohibits human trafficking, forced labor, and child labor
   - b. Article 24 - Prohibits children under 14 from working in factories, mines, or any hazardous jobs
4. Right to Freedom of Religion
   - a. Article 25-Freedom to profess, practice, and propagate any religion of their choice
   - b. Article 27
   - c. Article 28  - Government-funded schools are free of compulsory religious instruction
5. Cultural and Educational Rights
   - a. Article 29 - Right of any group of citizens to conserve their language, script or culture
   - b. Article 30 - Protect minorities; allow them to preserve language, culture, and education.
6. Right to Constitutional Remedies
   - a. Article 32 - “Heart of the Constitution” - Ability to approach Supreme Court or High Court directly to enforce their rights

## Directive Principles of State Policy

**Articles 36-51 Part IV**

- Social, Economic, and Political principles

1. Social Justice and Welfare
   - a. Article 39.(a) & (b)
      - i. Equity -> All citizens have adequate means of livelihood
      - ii. Education, healthcare, and nutrition
   - b. Article 41 - Provide work, education, and public assistance in cases of unemployment, old age, sickness, etc.
2. Economic democracy
   - a. Article 43, 43A - Promote cottage industries, co-operative societies, equal pay for men and women
   - b. Article 39(b) - no concentration of wealth
3. Gandhian Principles (Self-reliant India, agricultural)
   - a. Article 40 - Organize village panchayats for self-governance
   - b. Article 43, 48 - Agriculture, animal husbandry, protection of livestock and forests
4. Environmental and Ecological Principles
   - a. Article 48A - Protect environment, safeguard forests and wildlife; pollution control
5. Promotion of Education and Culture
   - a. Article 45,51A - Free and compulsory education for children, encourage learning
6. International Peace and Cooperation
   - a. Article 51 - Promote international peace
