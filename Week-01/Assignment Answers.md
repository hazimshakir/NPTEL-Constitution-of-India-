## Question 1

**The Indian Constitution is called a “living document” because:**

- A) It can be rewritten every year.
- B) It changes automatically without amendments.
- C) It can be amended to adapt to social, political, and economic changes.
- D) It applies only to citizens living in rural India.

**Correct Answer: C**

---

## Question 2

**Which of the following makes the Indian Constitution the “longest written Constitution in the world”?**

- A) It has over 470 Articles and 12 Schedules covering diverse governance issues.
- B) It includes only the Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles.
- C) It is written in multiple languages.
- D) It follows the British parliamentary system exclusively.

**Correct Answer: A**

---

## Question 3

**What does the “blend of rigidity and flexibility” in the Constitution imply?**

- A) All provisions can be changed by a simple majority in Parliament.
- B) Core provisions require special majority and state ratification, while others can be altered by a simple majority.
- C) The Constitution cannot be amended under any circumstances.
- D) Only the judiciary can amend the Constitution.

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Question 4

**In the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), what important doctrine did the Supreme Court establish?**

- A) Universal Adult Franchise
- B) Fundamental Duties
- C) Doctrine of Basic Structure
- D) Judicial Review is abolished

**Correct Answer: C — Doctrine of Basic Structure**

---

## Question 5

**Which of the following best explains why studying Constitutions is important?**

- A) They help citizens understand global trade laws.
- B) They establish the framework of governance and protect citizens' rights.
- C) They outline only judicial procedures.
- D) They are mainly meant to regulate international relations.

**Correct Answer: B — They establish the framework of governance and protect citizens' rights.**

## Question 6

**Who among the following was a woman member of the Constituent Assembly known for advocating gender equality and women's rights?**

- A) Hansa Mehta
- B) Annie Besant
- C) Indira Gandhi
- D) Sucheta Kripalani

**Correct Answer: A — Hansa Mehta**

## Question 7

**Which of these countries did not influence the Indian Constitution?**

- A) US Constitution
- B) Irish Constitution
- C) Canadian Constitution
- D) Soviet Union's October Manifesto

**Correct Answer: D — Soviet Union's October Manifesto**

---

## Question 8

**What does the phrase “We, the People of India” in the Preamble signify?**

- A) India is ruled by a king.
- B) Power belongs to the citizens of India.
- C) Only elected representatives have authority.
- D) India follows a theocratic system.

**Correct Answer: B — Power belongs to the citizens of India.**

---

## Question 9

**What does the term “Secular” in the Indian Preamble mean?**

- A) The state follows one official religion.
- B) The state discourages all religions.
- C) Equal respect for all religions.
- D) Religion has no place in public life.

**Correct Answer: C — Equal respect for all religions.**

---

## Question 10

**Which pair of words was added to the Preamble through the 42nd Constitutional Amendment in 1976?**

- A) Sovereign & Republic
- B) Secular & Socialist
- C) Liberty & Equality
- D) Justice & Fraternity

**Correct Answer: B — Secular & Socialist**
