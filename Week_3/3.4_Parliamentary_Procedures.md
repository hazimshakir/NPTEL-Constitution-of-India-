# Parliamentary Committees and Procedures

1. Parliamentary committees - Standing committees, and ad hoc committees

      ii. Most influential committees - Departmentally related standing committees, 24
         1. Ex : Finance, Home affairs, defence, education, health,...
      iii. Financial oversight - Public Accounts committee, Estimates committee, etc.
      iv. Examine bills
   b. Ad Hoc committee
      i. Temporary
   c. Ex : Personal data protection bill
      i. Joint parliamentary committee (ad hoc)
   d. Referral of bills to committees, strengthens legislative quality
