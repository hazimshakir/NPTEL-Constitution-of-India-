# Rajya Sabha “Council Of States”

1. Composition and structure
   - a. Maximum strength - 245 members
      - i. 233 members elected by the Legislative Assemblies of States and Union Territories
      - ii. 12 members nominated by the President for their distinguished contributions to arts, literature, science, social service, and public life
      - iii. Voting - Uttar Pradesh - 31, Tamil Nadu - 18, Sikkim-1 (population)
      - iv. Permanent and never dissolves completely
   - b. Members serve 6-year staggered terms with one-third retiring every 2 years
   - c. Vice President-ex-oﬃcio Chairman of Rajya Sabha
2. Powers and Functions
   - a. shares ordinary legislative powers equally on Union and Concurrent List subjects like education, forests, and marriage laws
   - b. Rajya Sabha has 14 days to send recommendations on money bill
   - c. initiate Constitutional Amendment Bills (Article 368) requiring special majority, participates in electing President and Vice President, approves All India Services creation, and ratifies emergency proclamations
   - d. Protects state interests through resolutions on Union territories and federal disputes
3. Election process
   - a. state assembly MLAs vote using single transferable vote with proportional representation where each MLA gets votes proportional to their state's Rajya Sabha seats
   - b. Candidates need minimum votes quota calculated as (total valid votes ÷ seats +1) and excess votes transfer to second preferences ensuring even small parties win
   - c. Biennial elections fill retiring seats
   - d. by-elections fill vacancies from resignations or disqualifications under 10th Schedule anti-defection law.
4. Procedural uniqueness:
   - a. Question Hour (140 questions/session vs Lok Sabha's 375)
   - b. Zero Hour for urgent matters
   - c. Short Duration Discussions
   - d. Calling AttentionMotions - Rajya Sabha pioneered 'Motion of Grave Concern' on national emergencies!
5. Special Powers
   - a. creating All India Services (IAS, IPS)
   - b. electoral college for President
   - c. ratifying international treaties
6. Fun Facts
   - a. Rajya Sabha's largest ever strength - 250 members
   - b. First woman member - Rajkumari Amrit Kaur (1950)
   - c. Longest serving member 50+ years by late V.V. Giri!
   - d. Current women representation - 11% (27 members)
