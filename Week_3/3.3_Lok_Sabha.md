# Lok Sabha

1. Lok Sabha - “House of the People”
   a. General election
      i. Up to 552 elected members
      ii. Some seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes
      iii. India is divided into parliamentary constituencies, each of which elects one Member of Parliament
      iv. General elections are conducted by the Election Commission of India, an independent constitutional authority.
2. Speaker
   a. Elected by the members of the Lok Sabha
   b. Constitutional powers
      i. Authority to certify whether a Bill is a money bill
      ii. Rulings are usually final
3. Law-making
   a. Financial power
      i. Union budget is presented
      ii. Tax cannot be imposed without approval
4. Zero hour, question house, debates, motions
5. No-confidence motion
