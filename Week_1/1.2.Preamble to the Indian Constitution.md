# Preamble to the Indian Constitution

**85 words**

## Revolutionary Manifesto

> “WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a
> SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its
> citizens: JUSTICE, social, economic and political; LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief,
> faith and worship; EQUALITY of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all
> FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation;
> IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this twenty-sixth day of November, 1949, do
> HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CONSTITUTION.”

## Core Ideas

- *Justice, Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity (JLEF)*
- *Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, and Democratic Republic (SSSDR)*

## Why the Preamble Matters Today

- December 13, 1946 — Jawaharlal Nehru stood before the Constituent Assembly and moved what he called the “Objective Resolution.”
- The original Preamble was adopted on November 26, 1949.
- The Constituent Assembly had 299 members.
- Dr. B. R. Ambedkar was the chief architect of the Constitution.
- The words “Socialist” and “Secular” were added later, in 1976, through the 42nd Amendment.

## Key Terms

- **“We, the people”** — sovereignty, or complete independence.
- **Socialist** — inspired by the Soviet model. It does not mean that the government controls all means of production; rather, it ensures that everyone has equal access to opportunities and resources.
- **Secular** — equal respect for all religions and no religious conflict.
- **Democratic** — government by the people and the right to vote.
- The President is chosen by representatives of the people.

## Justice Liberty Equality and Fraternity

- **Justice** — fairness to all.
- **Liberty** — freedom of thought, speech, belief, faith, and worship.
- **Equality** — no discrimination.
- **Fraternity** — brotherhood and unity.

## Judicial Importance

The **Kesavananda Bharati case (1973)** established that every law, judgment, and government policy must align with the values in the Preamble.
